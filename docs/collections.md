Editorial primary collections inferred from public event titles using reviewed, deterministic rules and explicit overrides. Each event appears in exactly one collection. Specific subject matter usually takes precedence over a social format; sports, wellbeing activities and audience-specific gatherings use their stated purpose. Broad titles are grouped by format or as open discovery. Collections are designed for manageable browsing, not equal-sized quotas, official organizer tags, or event quality rankings. A title alone may omit relevant subjects; consult the organizer before choosing an event.
