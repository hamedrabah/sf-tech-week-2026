# Plan your week

SF Tech Week runs **October 5–11, 2026**. Times in this guide are Pacific. This repository is a dated snapshot; use the [official SF calendar](https://www.tech-week.com/calendar/sf) for the latest listings and the organizer's registration page for final details.

## Register, then confirm

Each event has its own host and registration process. Open the event's public registration link, review its requirements, and apply or reserve a ticket. Hosts manage admission and follow up on registration status. See the [official attendance FAQ](https://www.tech-week.com/).

Keep **applied**, **waitlisted**, and **confirmed** separate in your own notes. A submitted form does not establish admission. Before leaving, check your confirmation, the current start time, ticket terms, and the venue supplied by the organizer. Keep confirmation messages, guest details, and private addresses out of public contributions.

## Leave room for the trip

The calendar covers the Bay Area. Its listings include San Francisco neighborhoods as well as Palo Alto, Mountain View, Stanford, and the East Bay. Check the actual location before treating two events as an easy pair.

Group confirmed events by area, allow time for check-in, and look up the route before adding another stop. Leave a gap after sessions where you want to meet people. For a workshop or hackathon, check equipment and setup requirements ahead of time.

## Keep the weekend in view
